# Warforge

Warforge is a contested region associated with repeated conflict between southern parts of the [[Republic of Arindus]] and [[Vaishen]].

Both powers claim or control portions of the wider area, but significant stretches function almost like no-man's-land and receive little consistent government.

Vaishen once made major territorial gains in the region.

Those advances slowed dramatically after the relevant old kingdom joined the Republic and gained the military, political, and economic backing of the larger federation.

Warforge remains one of the clearest modern examples of Republic-Vaishen rivalry.
