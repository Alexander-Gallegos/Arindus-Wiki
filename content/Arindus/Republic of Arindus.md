# Republic of Arindus

The Republic of Arindus is the largest and most influential nation on [[Arindus]]. It began roughly 90 years before the present, during the [[Demon Age]], when the already-famous [[Argent Circle]] convinced a group of rival kingdoms to unite against the growing demon threat.

The Republic was never originally intended to become a permanent nation. It began as a wartime coalition of roughly eight or nine kingdoms, then grew over the following years as worsening demon attacks, political pressure, protection, and further diplomacy drew additional kingdoms into the alliance. By the end of the Demon Age, roughly fifteen major kingdoms had joined.

When [[Magefall]] ended the Demon Age 80 years ago, the alliance remained together to manage refugees, reconstruction, security, and the reshaping of the continent. By the time the original crisis had passed, most of the rulers who had created the alliance were dead, their successors had grown accustomed to the shared system, and the kingdoms had become too economically, politically, and militarily interconnected for separation to be simple.

## Political Structure

The Republic is a federation of the old kingdoms. Most of those kingdoms still exist as distinct political entities with their own monarchies, laws, taxes, cultures, and regional governments.

Each kingdom chooses one representative to sit on the central council by whatever method that kingdom prefers. Most are monarchies, so the monarch either serves directly or appoints a representative.

Each representative also maintains an advisory council in the capital. Wealthy lesser nobles have significant influence over who sits on these advisory councils.

Ordinary citizens do not vote at the federal level.

Every member kingdom officially has equal representation regardless of population, wealth, or military strength, although those differences create enormous unofficial influence.

The council elects a Chancellor from outside the sitting kingdom representatives. Chancellors are usually high-ranking officials from the capital. A Chancellor serves for five years and may be retained for additional terms. A two-thirds vote of the kingdoms can remove a Chancellor early.

The Chancellor manages council debate, mediates disputes between kingdoms, breaks tied council votes, governs the capital through officials beneath the office, and serves as the senior civilian authority during an already-authorized military conflict. The Chancellor does not personally possess unlimited power to initiate military action; the council controls the use of Republic military force and may override the Chancellor.

## The Old Kingdoms

Roughly fifteen major old kingdoms make up the Republic.

Most remain culturally distinct, with differences closer to separate European nations than minor regional differences. Despite this, citizens increasingly view people from other member kingdoms as fellow countrymen, similar to a broad federal identity.

Regional identity remains extremely strong. Most citizens identify first with their home kingdom, while many people in and around the capital identify more strongly with the Republic itself.

Some former kingdoms no longer survive as functioning political entities.

[[Brahm]] is a special case. The Republic still claims the territory as part of its borders, while the people there live primarily in smaller independent tribal societies that largely ignore Republic authority. Neither side currently has enough interest in forcing the issue into a major war.

## Law and Citizenship

Citizenship primarily exists at the Republic level, but every person traveling through another member kingdom must obey that kingdom's local laws unless explicitly granted an exemption by high Republic or kingdom authority.

Internal borders are generally open. Individual kingdoms may refuse entry or expel people, though the central Republic can override those decisions.

Federal law prevents member kingdoms from legally conquering or overpowering one another. Economic pressure, noble influence, espionage, and other unofficial forms of competition still occur.

## Military

The Republic's military is highly integrated.

A recruit generally begins with roughly two years of training and service associated with their home kingdom. They are then required to spend roughly three years training and serving under Republic-wide standards.

Afterward, they may return to local kingdom defense and guard forces, join traveling Republic forces, or remain in the capital to pursue elite units, specialist roles, or the capital guard.

Although the military is structurally controlled by the Republic, soldiers often retain powerful loyalty to their home kingdom. If a kingdom ever attempted to secede, soldiers from that kingdom could be forced to choose between their Republic oath and their homeland, potentially splitting mixed units.

During peacetime, use of the Republic military is determined by the central council. During an active conflict, the Chancellor serves as the senior civilian authority but normally delegates battlefield command to professional military leaders. The council retains the right to override lower authority.

## Federal Institutions

The Republic directly maintains major courts, banks, intelligence services, military institutions, and government offices.

Universities, magical academies, libraries, and similar institutions are not officially controlled by the federal government, though powerful officials and organizations often exert unofficial influence.

[[Chrysus Arnkell]] serves as High Wizard and holds a unique place in the Republic's magical political structure. On paper, his political authority is lower than that of a kingdom representative. In practice, his reputation as a world-famous savior gives his words enormous influence.

## Economy

The Republic uses a standardized currency shared across much of the developed world. The standard originally spread through merchant networks because a common currency made international trade easier.

Some less-developed or isolated societies still barter or use local systems.

The [[Empire of Argile]] deliberately mints and controls its own currency, which exchanges against the wider trade standard.

## Language

Common is the dominant official language of the Republic and much of the world.

Its roots stretch back to the [[Draconic Age]], when a now-lost dominant civilization used a standardized language to communicate across its territories and with the many peoples under its influence. Modern Common is a descendant of that older language rather than an unchanged form of it.

The exact identity and nature of that ancient civilization are still being developed.

Regional dialects are common throughout the Republic, and some kingdoms may maintain secondary cultural languages.

## Society

The Republic is extremely diverse by the standards of the world and is generally viewed as one of its more open and opportunity-rich societies.

It also has severe structural inequality.

Noble classism, poverty, and social stratification remain widespread.

Slavery is legal throughout most or all of the Republic and is also common across much of the wider world. Halflings are legally citizens when free, but are widely treated as lesser people. Not all halflings are slaves, but a majority of enslaved people are halflings.

Free halflings technically possess legal equality, yet social discrimination makes true equality extremely rare. A halfling could theoretically become noble, but in practice this is almost unheard of. Noble children with halfling ancestry are often disowned or worse.

Tieflings also face major discrimination, including public fear, reduced opportunity, greater surveillance, and association with the demons of the Demon Age.

## Foreign Reputation

The Republic is a stable global superpower with immense political and military influence.

Different nations view it very differently:

- [[Zucriedor]] generally admires the Republic and sees it as an ally after receiving unofficial Republic help during its revolution.
- [[Halmsted]], [[Mebriele]], and [[Falstead]] often see it as a meddling superpower.
- [[Runma]] and [[Tyr]] tend to see it as chaotic.
- [[Whaik Shen]] and [[Fethioji]] retain tensions rooted partly in wars between Republic kingdoms and southern elven nations.
- [[Hecrar]] and [[Preid Pra]] often admire the Republic and seek its help against hostile neighbors.
- [[Astela]] largely keeps to itself.
- [[Shuyqua]] is a wealthy ally but often regards the Republic as comparatively chaotic and uncultured.
- [[Empire of Argile]] has poor relations with the Republic and much of the wider world.
- [[Priem Skijan]], the [[Wildlands]], and [[Priem Smaria]] are frequently isolated because their populations are widely discriminated against and stereotyped as monsters.
- [[Old Draconia]] is mysterious but somehow maintains broadly good relations with most powers.
- [[Oshea]] respects the Republic and, near the beginning of the story, seeks Republic aid during a war.
- The peoples of the [[Reladi Archipelago]] often regard the Republic as wealthy, intrusive, and annoying rather than as a central concern.

## Current Problems

At the beginning of the story, the Republic is stable but visibly imperfect.

Major public problems include:

- tensions with the [[Empire of Argile]]
- conflict with some southern high-elven powers
- economic inequality and classism
- recurring disagreements between member kingdoms
- the growing danger of the [[Magic Lands]]
- poverty and inadequate infrastructure in some regions
- the contradiction between the Republic's self-image as a land of opportunity and its acceptance of slavery and entrenched hierarchy

The Republic is intended to initially feel like a powerful, functional, comparatively free place with obvious moral and political flaws rather than either a utopia or a collapsing state.

The Republic's unnamed capital is by far the largest city in the world and the world's only true metropolis.
