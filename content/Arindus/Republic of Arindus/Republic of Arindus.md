# Republic of Arindus

The Republic of Arindus is the largest and most influential nation on Arindus.

It began roughly 90 years ago during the [[Demon Age]], when several rival kingdoms united against the growing demon threat. What began as a wartime alliance survived the end of the war and eventually became a permanent federation.

## Government

The Republic is made up of many old kingdoms that still retain their own cultures, laws, rulers, and regional identities.

Each kingdom is represented in the central government, while a Chancellor helps oversee the Republic as a whole.

## Society

The Republic is unusually diverse and is often viewed as a place of opportunity, especially compared with more isolated nations.

It is not an equal society. Wealth, nobility, slavery, regional identity, and species-based prejudice all have major effects on daily life.

## Military and Influence

The Republic possesses enormous military, political, economic, and magical influence.

Its size also creates constant disagreements between its member kingdoms and tensions with several neighboring powers.

## History

The [[Argent Circle]] played an important role in bringing the original kingdoms together during the Demon Age.

The alliance became permanent after [[Magefall]] forced the kingdoms to deal with reconstruction, refugees, security, and a changed world.

## Capital

The Republic's capital is the largest city in the known world and serves as the center of its federal government, trade, and many major institutions.

Characters from the Republic can receive more detailed information about their home kingdom or region during character creation.
