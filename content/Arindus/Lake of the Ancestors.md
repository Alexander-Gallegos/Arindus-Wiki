# Lake of the Ancestors

The Lake of the Ancestors is an enormous inland sea and one of the most sacred places in high-elven culture.

It is associated with the shared ancestry of [[Fethoji]], [[Vaishen]], and [[Astela]] and predates the political separation of their modern nations.

The lake is known to contain unusual magic, though the exact nature and origin of that power remain uncertain.

## Fethoji

The Elderwood primarily treat the lake as a sacred inheritance to be protected and preserved.

## Vaishen

The Highward believe the lake's gifts should be actively used rather than merely protected.

Some Vaishen scholars and traditions believe its power may even offer a way to reach, restore, or bring back ancient ancestors.

## Astela

The Stillveil continue to revere the lake despite their geographic isolation.

Astela periodically sends small pilgrimages or ceremonial delegations to the lake, which the other high-elven nations generally permit without conflict.
