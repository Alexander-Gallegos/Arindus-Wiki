# Hecrar

Hecrar is a comparatively young elven nation populated largely by **Stonewood Elves**.

It is older and more politically stable than [[Preid Pra]], but younger than the old high-elven states.

**Defining ideal:** Endurance

## Geography

Hecrar contains more open grassland than Preid Pra, with some mountain ranges and extensive coastline.

Its most distinctive natural resource is a species of grey-wood tree found throughout its forests.

This **Stonewood** is significantly stronger and sturdier than ordinary timber.

## The Stonewood Elves

Stonewood Elves are generally more settled, civic-minded, grounded, and people-oriented than the highly decentralized wood-elven tribes of Preid Pra.

Culturally, Hecrar sits somewhere between the independence of Preid Pra and the large, structured institutions of the high-elven nations.

The people tend to be practical and are comfortable using the resources nature provides rather than simply preserving them untouched.

Magic remains important, but Hecrar places less cultural emphasis on it than most other major elven nations.

## Government

Hecrar has a much more centralized government than Preid Pra.

The current working direction is an oligarchic government, likely built around major houses, regional powers, military leadership, trade interests, or some combination of them.

The exact structure remains open.

## Economy

Hecrar is decently wealthy, though nowhere near the great high-elven powers.

Stonewood is the foundation of much of that wealth.

The material is highly sought after because of its strength and durability, and Hecraran craftspeople understand how to work it better than anyone else.

Geographic distance from much of the wider world makes international trade more difficult than the country would prefer.

## Architecture and Military

Stonewood is central to Hecraran building, weapon-making, and defense.

Its structures can be unusually strong despite using large amounts of wood rather than stone.

Weapons made with Stonewood are difficult to damage or break.

This material advantage, combined with a serious and disciplined culture, contributes to a strong military.

## Capital

The capital is built from dark grey Stonewood mixed with stone construction.

It is heavily protected and visually serious rather than ornate.

The city gives an impression of strength, permanence, practicality, and order.

## Foreign Relations

Hecrar has friendly relations and regular trade with [[Preid Pra]].

Relations with [[Vaishen]] are cooler but functional. Trade continues despite Vaishen's tendency to regard non-high-elven peoples as lesser.

[[Fethoji]] and [[Astela]] generally view Hecrar positively as a legitimate elven nation descended from the remnants of the ancient continental elven world.

Hecrar has fairly good relations with the [[Republic of Arindus]] and is actively trying to strengthen them. One method is sending capable military combatants to assist Republic forces or allied causes when practical.

## Current Problems

Hecrar's greatest structural weakness is distance. Its remote position makes trade, diplomacy, and involvement in wider politics more difficult.

## Reputation

Hecrarans are often described as strong, stoic, straight to the point, serious, and sometimes cynical.
