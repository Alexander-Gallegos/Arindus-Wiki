# Vaishen

Vaishen is one of the three great high-elven successor states of the ancient continental elven civilization.

Its people are known as **the Highward**.

**Defining ideal:** Duty

## Geography

Vaishen is mountainous but also contains broad grasslands. It is notably less forested than most other elven nations.

It shares the [[Lake of the Ancestors]] with [[Fethoji]] and borders [[Preid Pra]].

## History

Vaishen and Fethoji trace their origins to the same ancient elven state. Their conflicting beliefs about what high-elven inheritance demanded of them were among the forces that helped fracture that civilization.

Fethoji emphasizes preservation.

Vaishen believes ancestral gifts exist to be used.

## Government

Vaishen is a unified mountain monarchy.

## The Highward

The Highward are proud high elves who value practicality, experience, action, discipline, and responsibility more than ceremonial beauty.

Their central cultural belief is that long life creates wisdom, and wisdom creates responsibility.

High elves live far longer than most peoples. Vaishen therefore argues that centuries of experience give high elves both the knowledge and the obligation to intervene when shorter-lived peoples repeatedly make destructive mistakes.

To the Highward, possessing ancestral wisdom and power while refusing to use it would dishonor the ancestors who gave those gifts.

This belief produces both genuine public service and strong cultural pride, but it can also justify domination.

## Social Hierarchy

Other high-elven peoples are treated as equals in ancestry even when Vaishen considers their philosophies weak or misguided.

Wood elves are treated much more harshly and are widely considered lesser or ungifted elves. Wood-elven slavery exists in Vaishen.

Other peoples are generally treated as nuisances or inferiors.

Humans face especially severe hostility because of the historical legacy of [[Marik Argile]] and the [[Empire of Argile]].

Foreign officials may receive somewhat better treatment, but relatively few non-elves live in Vaishen.

## Lake of the Ancestors

The [[Lake of the Ancestors]] is sacred to Vaishen.

The Highward believe Fethoji is wasting its potential by treating it only as something to preserve.

They believe the lake contains ancient magic and power that should be actively used. Some Vaishen traditions or scholars even believe it may be possible to call back or restore ancient elven ancestors through the lake.

The exact truth behind the lake remains unresolved.

## Economy

Vaishen is less wealthy than Fethoji.

Its economy includes agriculture, ore, and old generational wealth, but it trades less internationally than many major powers.

A very large share of national resources is consumed by warfare and military preparation.

## Military

Vaishen is famous for a highly skilled military.

Long-lived soldiers can accumulate decades or even centuries of training and experience, making individual Highward combatants particularly dangerous.

## Capital

The capital is built high in the mountains and extends partly into the stone itself.

Its architecture attempts to maintain high-elven grandeur but is more severe and practical than Fethoji's. Stone halls, carved mountain structures, military monuments, and exposed high plazas are common.

The city is extremely windy and commands dramatic views.

## Foreign Relations

Vaishen respects [[Fethoji]] and [[Astela]] as high-elven equals and tries to avoid direct war with either.

It repeatedly pressures Fethoji over the Lake of the Ancestors and often asks Astela to support its wars or disputes. Astela consistently refuses.

Vaishen has a hostile relationship with [[Preid Pra]] and historically treated its territory as land that could be crossed, raided, or exploited.

The [[Empire of Argile]] is a major ideological and historical enemy. Argile's human supremacy and Vaishen's high-elven supremacy are direct opposites.

Vaishen has also fought repeated wars against southern parts of the [[Republic of Arindus]], especially around [[Warforge]]. Vaishen gained ground for a time until the relevant old kingdom joined the Republic and gained far stronger backing.

## Current Problems

Vaishen's largest internal weakness is wealth. Continuous military activity consumes resources while the country participates relatively little in international trade.

## Reputation

Outsiders often describe the Highward as hardheaded, angry, fearsome, militaristic, and supremacist.

The Highward usually see themselves differently: as the people willing to accept the responsibility that others avoid.
