# Fethoji

Fethoji is one of the oldest surviving elven nations and one of the wealthiest countries in the world.

Its high-elven people are commonly known as **the Elderwood**.

**Defining ideal:** Elegance

## Geography

Fethoji is dominated by enormous forests, with mountain ranges and a significant stretch of grassland.

Along its border lies the [[Lake of the Ancestors]], a sacred site shared by the major high-elven peoples.

## History

Fethoji, [[Vaishen]], and [[Astela]] all descend from the ancient elven civilization that once united many elven peoples across the continent.

Fethoji emerged from the collapse of that older state as the branch most focused on preserving ancestral culture, dignity, and tradition.

The Elderwood generally view themselves as the truest continuation of old high-elven civilization.

## Government

Fethoji is governed through Elder Councils.

Age, ancestry, education, magical knowledge, and long-established families all carry substantial social weight.

## Society

Fethoji culture prizes grace, intelligence, beauty, ancestry, magical refinement, and stewardship of nature.

Its architecture is famous for white and emerald structures integrated into enormous ancient trees, ridges, waterfalls, and carefully cultivated woodland.

Non-elves are generally treated well by the standards of the wider world, but class divisions are obvious. High-elven families dominate much of the upper class, government, magical institutions, and old wealth.

## Magic and Nature

Magic is central to Fethoji society.

The nation lives among magical creatures, including fairies and other beings that are rare elsewhere.

Fethoji treats the [[Lake of the Ancestors]] primarily as something sacred to protect and preserve.

## Economy

Fethoji's wealth comes from a combination of ancient generational wealth, extraordinarily valuable land, long-lived families, and high-end exports.

Its current economy is strongly associated with luxury elven goods: exceptional bows, rare elven wood, magical materials, fine crafts, medicines, and other high-value items difficult to reproduce elsewhere.

## Capital

The capital stands along a wooded ridge and waterfall.

White and emerald buildings rise among enormous trees, including structures within the canopy. The city feels less constructed than cultivated over centuries.

## Foreign Relations

Fethoji has a generally good relationship with [[Astela]] and regards the Stillveil as worthy and refined high elves.

Its relationship with [[Vaishen]] is much more strained. Vaishen repeatedly pressures Fethoji over control and use of the [[Lake of the Ancestors]], while Fethoji sees the Highward as overly aggressive and reckless.

Fethoji avoids direct war with Vaishen when possible because both peoples still recognize one another as high-elven equals.

Relations with the [[Republic of Arindus]] are neither especially good nor especially bad. Fethoji considers the Republic useful for trade but often views it as chaotic, lacking dignity, and unlikely to remain unified forever.

The [[Empire of Argile]] is an old enemy. Argilian forces successfully occupied parts of Fethoji in the distant past but never maintained lasting control.

## Current Problems

Major concerns include Vaishen's continued pressure over the Lake of the Ancestors and outsiders attempting to poach magical creatures or steal valuable elven materials.

## Reputation

Outsiders often describe the Elderwood as rich, uptight, elegant, and snobbish.
