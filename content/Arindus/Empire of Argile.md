# Empire of Argile

The **Empire of Argile** is one of the oldest and most historically important human powers in [[Arindus]]. It occupies the human ancestral homeland and remains a formidable state despite having lost most of the territory once held by the old empire.

## Geography

The Empire is geographically varied. Much of it is flatland and forest surrounded by mountain ranges, with colder territory in the south. Its northern reaches include drier, less fertile land, but the region remains broadly livable.

## Human Homeland

Humans originate in the region now controlled by the Empire. The Empire therefore carries an unusually strong connection between national identity and human history.

The Empire's older history is not simply a history of conquest. During [[Marik Argile]]'s campaigns against the dragons, many territories joined him willingly, while others were conquered, absorbed through political pressure, or united through dynastic ties.

At its height, the old Empire stretched through lands now associated with [[Zucriedor]], [[Reladi]], the [[Reladi Archipelago]], [[Oshea]], [[Brahm]], the [[Republic of Arindus]], [[Mebriele]], and [[Halmsted]].

Marik also married a ruler who controlled a large portion of what is now Republic territory, joining their lands together.

After Marik's death, the Empire fragmented among his children. Over generations, the branches of that divided empire lost control for many different reasons until only one major branch remained as the modern Empire of Argile.

The spread of the old Empire is one of the major reasons humans are now found across so much of the world, and one reason the modern Republic is so culturally diverse.

## Marik Argile

[[Marik Argile]] is revered throughout the Empire to an extraordinary degree. He is remembered as a mortal human rather than a god, but his cultural importance can approach religious devotion.

The oldest Argilian ideal associated with Marik centers on liberation from tyrants, especially the defeat and banishment of the dragons.

Over later generations, that legacy was increasingly reinterpreted as proof of human superiority. Recent rulers pushed openly human-supremacist policies and discrimination.

## Government

The Empire is a hereditary imperial monarchy. The Emperor delegates many smaller decisions to officials, but the throne possesses ultimate authority.

To the outside world, the current Emperor is still believed to be the father of [[Canin Argile]]. The Empire largely closed itself off several years before the beginning of the campaign, and the lack of reliable information has caused widespread speculation.

Most foreign observers assume the Emperor is rebuilding after previous failures, consolidating power, or preparing for future conflict. Many people outside the Empire do not even know he has a son.



## Military

The Empire has long been known for military strength and for historically fighting wars on several fronts at once.

Its armed forces remain formidable even after centuries of territorial losses.

## Economy

The Empire is internally organized and relatively self-sufficient, but integrates poorly into the wider world economy.

It uses its own currency rather than the common international standard, both as a symbol of sovereignty and as a way for the state to maintain tighter economic control.

## Capital

The capital is built into a mountain. It is dense, busy, stone-heavy, and visually darker than many major capitals.

Crimson and navy blue are common colors. Wealthier noble districts include conspicuous gold decoration.

Statues of former rulers are common, with an enormous statue of [[Marik Argile]] dominating the city's historical imagery.

## Foreign Relations

The Empire has few true allies.

Its deepest current hostilities include [[Zucriedor]], [[Fethoji]], and [[Vaishen]]. Its relationship with the [[Republic of Arindus]] is also poor, especially because the Republic is one of the few powers large enough to openly resist Argilian influence.

Relations with [[Reladi]] and [[Sosmuila]] are somewhat less hostile than with many states, partly because their governments share some authoritarian tendencies, but this does not make them close allies.

[[Astela]] dislikes the Empire's human-supremacist legacy but is not currently driven by a strong active grudge.

## Reputation

Common foreign stereotypes describe Argilians as racist, supremacist, authoritarian, and obsessed with restoring the old empire.

The truth varies greatly by era and ruler.
