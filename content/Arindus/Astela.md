# Astela

Astela is one of the oldest surviving high-elven nations and one of the most magically advanced countries in the world.

Its people are known as **the Stillveil**.

**Defining ideal:** Calm

## Geography

Astela lies in extremely harsh southern terrain dominated by mountains, tundra forests, deep snow, and bitter cold.

Conditions that are dangerous or unbearable to most peoples are ordinary to the Stillveil.

The homeland itself is one reason Astela has remained difficult to invade and easy to leave alone.

## History

Astela separated from the ancient continental elven civilization during the same broad era that produced [[Fethoji]] and [[Vaishen]].

Rather than commit to the ideological conflict between the other high-elven branches, the ancestors of Astela withdrew into their own severe homeland and focused inward.

## Government

Astela is a monarchy ruled by a Queen.

## The Stillveil

The Stillveil are deeply connected to the magic within their own blood and the natural world around them.

They are usually slow to anger, level-headed, restrained, and uninterested in proving their superiority to outsiders.

Where Fethoji seeks to preserve its inheritance and Vaishen seeks to use it, Astela seeks to understand it.

Magic and nature are the two strongest foundations of Stillveil culture.

## Magic

Magic is more important in Astela than in nearly any other nation.

Since the destruction of the great magical nation at [[Magefall]], Astela may be the strongest surviving magical country in the world.

The magical destruction surrounding Magefall affected magic and nature across the world and remains one of the few outside events powerful enough to seriously concern Astela.

## Outsiders

Astela does not see many foreigners.

Those who manage to reach the capital are often treated with unusual respect because surviving the journey suggests exceptional strength, resilience, magical ability, divine favor, or some combination of them.

The nation's isolation is therefore more geographic and philosophical than openly xenophobic.

## Economy and Wealth

Astela participates relatively little in ordinary international economics.

It may possess enormous magical resources, crystals, ancient objects, enchanted materials, and other things outsiders would consider priceless, while having relatively little need for conventional wealth.

## Capital

The capital stands high in the frozen mountains.

Stone and crystal buildings rise among enormous magical iceworks and sculptures. A giant crystal palace dominates the city.

Despite the wind and cold, the capital is remarkably quiet, at times almost silent.



## Lake of the Ancestors

Astela still considers the [[Lake of the Ancestors]] deeply important.

At certain times, possibly tied to a religious or ancestral holiday, Astela sends a small delegation or pilgrimage to the lake.

Fethoji and Vaishen allow these visits because of their good relations with Astela and their shared recognition of the lake's sacred importance.

## Foreign Relations

Astela and [[Fethoji]] get along very well. Fethoji views the Stillveil as elegant and worthy high elves, while Astela respects the Elderwood's connection to nature and generally quiet approach.

Astela avoids open conflict with [[Vaishen]]. Vaishen repeatedly asks for Astela's magical aid, while Astela repeatedly refuses.

Privately, many Stillveil view the Highward as violent and disrespectful toward their ancestry, but Astela rarely says this publicly because it sees little value in creating unnecessary conflict.

Astela views the [[Republic of Arindus]] as chaotic and disorganized but also as a valiant attempt to unite very different peoples.

It views the modern [[Empire of Argile]] as a fallen version of a nation that no longer understands what it once stood for.

## Current Problems

Astela faces continuing pressure from outsiders who want it to become more involved in world affairs.

It is also deeply concerned by the long-term magical and natural consequences of Magefall.

## Reputation

Depending on the culture, the Stillveil are described as cold, heartless, gentle, kind, emotionless, sacred, mysterious, or almost like living messengers of nature.
