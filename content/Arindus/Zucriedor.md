# Zucriedor

Zucriedor is the newest major independent nation in [[Arindus]], having broken away from the [[Empire of Argile]] roughly fifteen years before the present.

**Defining ideal:** Freedom

## Geography

Zucriedor is tropical, wet, and heavily covered in jungle. Its soil is exceptionally rich and supports abundant plant and animal life. Mountain ranges break up otherwise lush lowlands.

The country is known for exotic fruits, animals, medicinal and natural resources, and other goods produced by its unusually fertile environment.

## Before Independence

Under Argilian rule, Zucriedor was treated as one of the poorer and less-developed parts of the Empire.

Large amounts of local resources were sent elsewhere while relatively little wealth returned to ordinary communities. Many working families lived in small, poorly insulated one-room homes shared by large households.

Resistance to Argilian rule existed long before the formal revolution. Battles, uprisings, and clashes occurred for years before the conflict finally developed into a full national revolution.

## The Revolution

The revolution began in earnest while [[Reidin Maxwell]] was still young.

Most revolutionary fighters were ordinary people rather than professional soldiers. This history remains central to the national identity.

Maxwell became one of the revolution's most famous figures and is still widely regarded as a founding hero of independent Zucriedor.

He refuses repeated calls to take formal political control and has not returned to live in Zucriedor.



## Government

Zucriedor is a young parliamentary democracy.

The government is reasonably stable, but the country is still changing rapidly and building institutions that did not exist under Argilian rule.

One recurring political issue is the continued public desire for Maxwell to take leadership despite his refusal.

## The Republic Debate

The [[Republic of Arindus]] has played a major role in helping Zucriedor stabilize through trade, political support, institutional advice, and international recognition.

This relationship is also one of the country's biggest political debates.

Some Zucriedorans see the Republic as a trusted ally and an example worth following.

Others appreciate the help but believe dependence must decrease as quickly as possible.

A more suspicious group argues that a nation which fought to escape one great power should be extremely careful about allowing another superpower too much influence.

The argument is not primarily over whether the Republic is an enemy, but over how much foreign help can be accepted before help becomes influence.

## Economy

Zucriedor possesses enormous natural wealth but is still developing the infrastructure needed to fully benefit from it.

Its most recognizable exports include exotic fruits, animals, agricultural goods, and jungle resources.

The nation still depends significantly on trade and support from the Republic, though that dependence has decreased since independence.

## Capital

The capital grew from an old revolutionary base.

It remains relatively small and has uneven infrastructure. Many important buildings began as repurposed revolutionary structures rather than ancient government halls or palaces.

The city has no major defensive walls; the surrounding jungle itself provides substantial protection.

## Foreign Relations

The [[Empire of Argile]] is widely hated and associated with dictatorship, exploitation, and violence.

The [[Republic of Arindus]] is Zucriedor's most important ally and trading partner.

Zucriedor also has generally positive relations with [[Astela]], [[Oshea]], and some tribes of the [[Reladi Archipelago]].

Relations with other tribes in the archipelago can be hostile. Tensions also exist with [[Vaishen]] and possibly [[Reladi]].

## Reputation

Outsiders often stereotype Zucriedorans as disorganized, savage, or little more than guerrilla fighters.

Zucriedorans tend to see themselves as people who survived exploitation, fought for their own country, and now have to learn how to keep the freedom they won.
