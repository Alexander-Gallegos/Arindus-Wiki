# Argent Circle

The Argent Circle was the legendary hero party that stood at the forefront of the [[Demon Age]] and ultimately helped bring it to an end.

The group had already been famous for years before the Republic formed.

Its members fought together for roughly fifteen years and built relationships with rulers and nations across the continent.

Those relationships allowed the Argent Circle to bring rival kingdoms into the same room when the demon threat became impossible to ignore.

Their diplomacy directly led to the creation of the [[Republic of Arindus]] roughly 90 years before the present.

Known members include:

- [[Chrysus Arnkell]]
- an unnamed nonmagical martial member later blamed for [[Magefall]]

## The Nonmagical Member

One member of the Argent Circle possessed no magic.

In a world that historically treated magic users as more capable and prestigious, he became a public symbol for martial fighters who believed nonmagical warriors deserved equal respect.

He argued that magical ability did not make a person inherently superior.

His movement became large enough that his name was closely associated with martial equality across the world.

He and Chrysus Arnkell were once best friends.

## Magefall and Public Legacy

Official history says the nonmagical member eventually betrayed the Argent Circle, sided with the demonic cause, and destroyed the magical nation during [[Magefall]] in order to prove that a martial fighter could stand above mages.

He is now overwhelmingly remembered as a mass murderer and fallen hero.

His earlier philosophy remains influential.

Some modern martial-equality advocates defend his original message while condemning what he is believed to have done.

Extremists sometimes call him a martyr or argue that his actions were justified.

The Republic has repeatedly suppressed groups that become violent or organize around glorifying him.

## Monument

A famous monument to the Argent Circle stands in [[Capital of the Republic]].

It was built before the Demon Age ended.

The figure representing the member later blamed for Magefall was removed rather than destroyed.

The monument therefore contains a deliberate, conspicuous empty space.

That absence has become almost as recognizable as the surviving figures.

Some people vandalize the empty section.

Others periodically try to restore or symbolically add the missing hero back.
