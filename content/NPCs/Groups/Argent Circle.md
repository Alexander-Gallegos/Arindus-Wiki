# Argent Circle

The Argent Circle was the legendary hero party that stood at the forefront of the [[Demon Age]].

The group had already been famous for years before the [[Republic of Arindus]] formed and fought together for roughly fifteen years.

Their connections with rulers across the continent helped bring rival kingdoms together against the demon threat, contributing directly to the creation of the Republic.

## Magefall

One member of the group possessed no magic and had become a famous advocate for the idea that martial fighters deserved the same respect as mages.

During the final events of the Demon Age, he betrayed the Argent Circle and is blamed for destroying the magical nation during [[Magefall]].

He is remembered as a fallen hero and mass murderer, while the ideals of martial equality that he once represented remain influential.

## Legacy

A famous monument to the Argent Circle stands in the Republic's capital. The betrayer's figure was removed, leaving a conspicuous empty place in the monument.

[[Chrysus Arnkell]] is publicly remembered as the only surviving loyal member of the group.
