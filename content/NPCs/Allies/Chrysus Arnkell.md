# Chrysus Arnkell

Known throughout the world as the Great Wizard Chrysus Arnkell.

Arnkell was a member of the legendary [[Argent Circle]], the hero party that spent roughly fifteen years fighting together and eventually brought the [[Demon Age]] to an end.

He is publicly regarded as the only surviving loyal member of the Argent Circle after [[Magefall]].

He remains one of the most trusted and famous living people in the world.

## Republic Role

Arnkell serves as High Wizard of the [[Republic of Arindus]].

He participates in a special magical advisory structure attached to the Republic government.

On paper, his direct political power is lower than that of a kingdom representative.

In practice, his reputation as a world-saving hero gives his words extraordinary weight with the council, officials, mages, soldiers, nobles, and ordinary citizens.

Very few people seriously suspect him of wrongdoing.

## Magical Academy

Arnkell is the head of one of the Republic's most prestigious magical academies, located within the castle complex of [[Capital of the Republic]].

He oversees its major direction and decisions but does not personally manage ordinary daily administration.

Students may occasionally see him on campus, though he spends much of his time on government, research, and other responsibilities.



## Argent Circle Betrayer

Arnkell and the unnamed nonmagical Argent Circle member later blamed for Magefall were once best friends.

The other member had become a major public champion for martial equality in a world that often treated nonmagical fighters as inherently less capable than mages.

Official history says that member ultimately betrayed the Argent Circle and caused the destruction of the magical nation.

Arnkell defeated and sealed him at the old magical capital.

The prisoner was later moved beneath the Republic capital and is now held at the deepest level of the prison under the castle.

Arnkell carries a necklace that sustains the magical seal keeping him contained.

The exact catastrophic act the prisoner was attempting to complete if released remains undecided.
