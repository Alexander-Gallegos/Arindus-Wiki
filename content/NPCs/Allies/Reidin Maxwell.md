# Reidin Maxwell

Reidin Maxwell, widely known as **The Lucky One**, is one of the most famous living heroes associated with the independence of [[Zucriedor]].

As a child, Maxwell lost his parents, grandparents, and seven siblings when Argilian wizards destroyed his home during fighting. He was seven years old and the only survivor.

That survival became the origin of the name **The Lucky One**.

Maxwell later became one of the most famous fighters of the Zucriedor revolution.

Many people in Zucriedor still want him to take formal political leadership. Maxwell refused and eventually left the country rather than become its ruler.

He now lives and works in the [[Republic of Arindus]].
