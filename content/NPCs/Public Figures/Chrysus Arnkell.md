# Chrysus Arnkell

Chrysus Arnkell, widely known as the Great Wizard, is one of the most famous living people in the world.

He was a member of the legendary [[Argent Circle]] and is publicly remembered as the group's only surviving loyal member after [[Magefall]].

## Republic Role

Arnkell serves as High Wizard of the [[Republic of Arindus]].

His formal political authority is limited, but his reputation as a hero gives him enormous influence throughout the Republic.

## Magical Academy

Arnkell is the head of one of the Republic's most prestigious magical academies, located in the capital.

He oversees its major direction while leaving most ordinary administration to others.

## The Argent Circle

Arnkell and the nonmagical Argent Circle member later blamed for Magefall were once close friends.

Their relationship and eventual conflict are among the most famous personal stories connected to the end of the Demon Age.
