# Draconic Age

The Draconic Age was an ancient period when dragons held immense power and influence across the world.

Much of the age is known through old histories, ruins, surviving traditions, and later retellings.

[[Marik Argile]] is remembered as the figure who brought the age to an end and later founded the [[Empire of Argile]].

The exact details of such an ancient period vary between historical and cultural traditions.
