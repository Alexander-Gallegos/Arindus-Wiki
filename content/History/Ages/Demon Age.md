# Demon Age

A period in the current history of Arindus when demons began emerging from the [[Hells]] and attacking mortal civilizations across the world.

As the demon problem grew beyond what individual kingdoms could contain, the famous hero party known as the [[Argent Circle]] used its connections and reputation to bring rival rulers together.

Roughly 90 years before the present, their diplomacy produced the first form of the [[Republic of Arindus]].

The Republic began as a temporary military alliance of roughly eight or nine kingdoms and was never expected to survive permanently. Over the following years, additional kingdoms joined because of worsening demon attacks, pressure from neighboring powers, the protection offered by the alliance, and further intervention by the Argent Circle.

By the later Demon Age, roughly fifteen major kingdoms were united.

The age ended 80 years before the present with [[Magefall]], a sudden catastrophe centered on the magical nation and the site where the demonic invasion had nearly succeeded.
