# Magefall

Magefall was the catastrophe that ended the [[Demon Age]] roughly 80 years ago.

The great magical nation was destroyed with very little warning, leaving behind the devastated region now associated with the [[Magic Lands]].

The disaster is blamed on the nonmagical member of the [[Argent Circle]], who betrayed the group during the final events of the Demon Age.

Magefall caused an enormous loss of life and magical knowledge and remains one of the defining events of modern history.
