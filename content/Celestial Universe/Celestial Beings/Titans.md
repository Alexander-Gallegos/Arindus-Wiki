The Titans are described in ancient traditions as three of the original [[Celestial Beings]].

One major creation tradition teaches that the Titans sacrificed themselves to contain the [[Bleeding Monarch]] at the end of [[The Celestial Conflict]], and that the [[Mortal Realms]] formed from their remains.

Druids and goliath traditions generally treat the Titans as far more than distant myth, though beliefs about their exact history vary.
