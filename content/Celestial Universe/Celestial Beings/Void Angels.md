The other "children" of [[Chaos]] born it's interaction with [[God of Void]]. These are beings that live in the void between all realms known as "The Leaves of the [[Alltree]]". 

They are the guides and guardians between realms. They are the first line of defense against those who wish to invade the heavenly or hellish realms. Although they will abandon any realms in order to protect the [[Alltree]] and [[Chaos]]. 

Along with their master [[God of Void]], they align with no covenants, no gods, no beings except for [[Chaos]].
