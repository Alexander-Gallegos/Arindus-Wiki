The Celestial Conflict is one of the central events in the world's major creation traditions.

A dominant religious account describes it as the first war between the [[Bleeding Monarch]] and the [[Titans]]. In that telling, the strain upon [[Chaos]] caused it to shatter, the first [[Gods]] emerged and divided into two [[Covenants]], and the Titans ultimately sacrificed themselves to deny the Bleeding Monarch their power and imprison him.

That same tradition teaches that the [[Mortal Realms]] came from the Titans' sacrifice. Other cultures and religions may preserve different versions of these ancient events.
