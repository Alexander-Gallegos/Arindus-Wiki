H1

The Celestial Conflict was the first war ever waged between the [[Bleeding Monarch]] and [[Titans]]. 

This was caused after the [[Bleeding Monarch]] caused so much tension on [[Chaos]] that it shattered allowing for access to the [[Titans]]. The shattering created the first [[Gods]] who quickly took sides in the conflict separating into two [[Covenants]]. After the [[Titans]] foresaw their defeat, they decided to sacrifice themselves in order to prevent the [[Bleeding Monarch]] from obtaining their power, and trapping him. In doing so they created the [[Mortal Realms]], leaving the [[Gods]] to rule and dictate the universe that began to expand.
