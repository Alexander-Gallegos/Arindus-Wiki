# Covenant of Blood

The Covenant of Blood is the name commonly given to the Gods remembered as siding with the [[Bleeding Monarch]] during the ancient divine conflict.

Commonly associated members include:

**Primals**
- God of Knowledge
- God of Ambition
- God of Creation
- Goddess of Desire
- Goddess of Freedom

**Later Gods**
- God of Fate
- God of Motion
- God of Shadow
- God of Space
- Goddess of Form
- Goddess of Change

Different religions disagree strongly about how these Gods should be understood or worshipped.
