# Countries at a Glance

Arindus contains many nations, cultures, and political systems.

This page is a starting point rather than a complete guide.

## Major Powers and Regions

### [[Republic of Arindus]]

The largest and most influential nation on Arindus.

It began as an alliance of kingdoms during the [[Demon Age]] and later became a permanent federation. Its old kingdoms still retain strong identities, laws, governments, and cultures.

### [[Empire of Argile]]

A major power with poor relations with the Republic and much of the wider world.

### [[Zucriedor]]

A relatively young independent country that gained freedom from the Empire of Argile. Its rebellion is strongly associated with [[Reidin Maxwell]].

### [[Runma]]

A country built around one of the [[World Trees]].

### [[Magic Lands]]

The remains of the magical nation destroyed during [[Magefall]].

### [[Old Draconia]]

A mysterious region connected to the legacy of the [[Draconic Age]].

### [[Reladi]] and [[Reladi Archipelago]]

Important regions with their own distinct cultures and relationships with the Republic.
