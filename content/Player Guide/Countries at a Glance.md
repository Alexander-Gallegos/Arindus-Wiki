# Countries at a Glance

Arindus contains many nations, cultures, and political systems.

These summaries are intentionally broad. A character who chooses a particular homeland can receive more detailed knowledge that someone from that place would reasonably know.

## Major Powers and Regions

### [[Republic of Arindus]]
The largest and most influential nation on Arindus. It began as an alliance of kingdoms during the [[Demon Age]] and later became a permanent federation. Its old kingdoms still retain strong regional identities.

### [[Empire of Argile]]
A major power with a long history and difficult relations with the Republic and several other nations.

### [[Zucriedor]]
A comparatively young independent country that gained its freedom from the Empire of Argile.

### [[Runma]]
A nation built around the immense sacred [[World Trees|World Tree of Arindus]].

### [[Magic Lands]]
The devastated remains of the magical nation destroyed during [[Magefall]].

### [[Old Draconia]]
A mysterious region strongly associated with the legacy of the [[Draconic Age]].

### [[Reladi]] and [[Reladi Archipelago]]
Distinct regions with their own cultures and histories.
