# Creating a Character in Arindus

You do not need to know the whole setting before making a character.

The easiest way to begin is to decide where your character is from, what kind of life they had, and how much they know about the wider world.

## 1. Choose a Homeland

Start with [[Player Guide/Countries at a Glance|Countries at a Glance]].

The [[Republic of Arindus]] is the largest and most influential nation and contains many old kingdoms with strong regional identities.

Other places include nations such as [[Empire of Argile]], [[Zucriedor]], [[Runma]], [[Reladi]], [[Shuyqua]], [[Old Draconia]], and many others.

Your homeland can strongly shape your character's politics, culture, education, prejudices, religion, and worldview.

## 2. Think About Their Relationship With Magic

Magic is an important part of the world.

A character may be highly educated in magic, barely understand it, distrust it, admire it, or simply see it as normal.

See [[Player Guide/Magic in Everyday Life|Magic in Everyday Life]].

## 3. Think About Recent History

The [[Demon Age]] ended roughly 80 years ago with [[Magefall]].

That means the consequences are still recent enough to affect governments, families, institutions, religion, and public attitudes.

See [[Player Guide/Recent History|Recent History]].

## 4. Think About Religion

The world contains many [[Gods]] and broader traditions surrounding them.

A character might worship one god closely, honor many gods, follow local traditions, or care very little about religion.

See [[Player Guide/Religion and the Gods|Religion and the Gods]].

## 5. Think About Social Position

The world contains major differences in wealth, class, citizenship, species, and political power.

Some characters may come from nobility or powerful institutions, while others may come from poverty, slavery, isolated communities, military service, criminal backgrounds, or ordinary working families.

See [[Player Guide/Species and Society|Species and Society]].

## 6. You Do Not Need Perfect Lore Knowledge

Your character does not need to know everything you know as a player.

Once characters are created, some may receive additional background information based on their homeland, education, profession, religion, or personal history.
