# Magic in Everyday Life

Magic is an important and visible part of life in Arindus.

The world has historically placed significant value on magical ability, though magic is not universal.

## Magic and Status

In many societies, people with magic have traditionally been viewed as more capable, prestigious, or important than people without it.

That attitude is not universal, and it has created social and political tension.

The history of the [[Argent Circle]] includes a famous nonmagical hero who became strongly associated with the idea that martial fighters deserved equal respect.

## Magefall

A great deal of magical knowledge was lost during [[Magefall]].

The destruction of the magical nation changed magical education and scholarship across the world.

## Magical Institutions

The [[Republic of Arindus]] contains powerful magical institutions.

[[Chrysus Arnkell]] is one of the world's most famous living mages and serves as the Republic's High Wizard.

## Character Creation

A character who studies magic may know much more than the average person.

Once characters are created, characters with magical education may receive additional information that is not part of this general introduction.
