# Species and Society

Arindus contains many peoples, cultures, and species.

Social treatment varies heavily by nation and region.

## The Republic

The [[Republic of Arindus]] is extremely diverse and is generally seen as comparatively open and opportunity-rich.

It also contains major inequality and discrimination.

## Halflings

In much of the Republic, slavery is legal.

Halflings are legally citizens when free, but they are widely treated as socially inferior, and a large portion of enslaved people are halflings.

Free halflings may possess legal rights on paper while still facing severe discrimination in practice.

## Tieflings

Tieflings also face major prejudice.

Their appearance is often associated with demons and the [[Demon Age]], which can lead to fear, discrimination, reduced opportunity, and increased suspicion.

## Class and Wealth

Nobility, poverty, class divisions, and political influence vary greatly across the world.

Where a character comes from can matter as much as their species.

## Character Creation

Your character's homeland, wealth, family, species, education, and social position can all shape what parts of the world they understand and how other people respond to them.
