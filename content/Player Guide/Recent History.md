# Recent History

The modern world is heavily shaped by two major historical periods: the [[Draconic Age]] and the [[Demon Age]].

## Draconic Age

The Draconic Age belongs to an older period of history when dragons held extraordinary power and influence.

[[Marik Argile]] is remembered as the founder of the [[Empire of Argile]] and as the figure credited with bringing the Draconic Age to an end.

## Demon Age

The [[Demon Age]] was a period when demons emerged from the [[Hells]] and attacked mortal civilizations across the world.

The already-famous [[Argent Circle]] played a major role in resisting them and in bringing rival kingdoms together. Those alliances eventually became the [[Republic of Arindus]].

## Magefall

The Demon Age ended roughly 80 years ago with [[Magefall]].

Magefall destroyed the great magical nation with very little warning and left behind the devastated region now associated with the [[Magic Lands]].

The disaster remains one of the defining events of modern history.

## The Argent Circle

The [[Argent Circle]] is remembered as the legendary hero party that helped end the Demon Age.

One of its nonmagical members betrayed the group during the events surrounding Magefall and is blamed for the destruction of the magical nation.

[[Chrysus Arnkell]] is publicly remembered as the only surviving loyal member of the group.
