# Recent History

The modern world is heavily shaped by two major historical periods: the [[Draconic Age]] and the [[Demon Age]].

## Draconic Age

The [[Draconic Age]] belongs to an older period of history.

[[Marik Argile]] is remembered as the founder of the [[Empire of Argile]] and as the person credited with bringing the Draconic Age to an end.

## Demon Age

The [[Demon Age]] was a period when demons emerged from the [[Hells]] and attacked mortal civilizations.

As the threat grew, the already-famous [[Argent Circle]] used its influence to bring rival kingdoms together.

That wartime alliance eventually became the [[Republic of Arindus]].

## Magefall

The Demon Age ended roughly 80 years ago with [[Magefall]].

Magefall destroyed the great magical nation and left behind the region now associated with the [[Magic Lands]].

The event remains one of the defining disasters of modern history.

## The Argent Circle

The [[Argent Circle]] is remembered as the legendary hero party that helped end the Demon Age.

Its members were famous before the Republic existed and were instrumental in bringing rival rulers together.

[[Chrysus Arnkell]] remains one of the most famous figures associated with the group.
